## Required validation package

Provide a dated NZP engineering-approved mass/energy balance and economic workbook for each feedstock/country combination. Record as-received versus dry tonne basis, composition, moisture and scale limits, normal gas conditions, product recovery and purity, uptime, utility demand, residual handling and yield uncertainty. Economics require currency/date, selected product pathway, product price and gate fee, total installed project CAPEX and complete annual OPEX. Carbon savings require an approved counterfactual and lifecycle boundary, including electricity, fossil/biogenic carbon, downstream use and avoided-disposal assumptions.

Syngas composition (47.4% H₂ and 47.3% CO in the readiness summary) cannot be used as mass yield. Do not infer annual availability, gas heating value, recovery efficiency, prices or carbon intensity. No missing input is represented as zero.

## Approval procedure

Import private source text into the authenticated admin area. An authorised NZP reviewer records disclosure permission, canonical citation URL and review rationale before approval. Approval creates searchable embeddings; only approved/public evidence is retrieved. Approve a coefficient set only after all factors and validity assumptions have been reviewed against its approved source. Editing or revoking that source removes dependent approvals. Preserve the original signed workbook as the reference record.
