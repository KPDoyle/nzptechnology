## Project and technical sources reviewed

- `CASE Studies.docx`: Lancaster metals recovery, historical period 1998–mid-2000s; 1 MW submerged-arc system; modular fabrication, separate metals/slag tapping and combusted offgas. Later mothballing/dismantling stated so it is not represented as a current plant.
- Same source: Huntsville programme, 1998–mid-2000s; 1 MW AC copper-electrode plasma torch; contraband and test-feedstock work, with gas combustion in destruction duty. Source is a retrospective team case history, not a new independent audit.
- `HANJUNG-99_HJ-200 Brochure 2025 1.pdf`: HVP200 brochure, treatment system at KAERI, two graphite electrodes, 200 kg/h treatment design, integrated offgas and control systems, structured testing. Archival filename is not treated as the date of the pilot programme.
- `ASPEN model report.pdf`: INL/LTD-15-36850, October 2015, Michael McKellar, Nick Soelberg and Tom Eddy. Assumed input 500 tonnes/day; Table 1 on printed page 5 gives 0.485 H2 and 0.485 CO (mole fraction). Section 2.2 separately reports a water-containing stream with a different composition. Website preserves the Table 1 basis without asserting dry basis, measured purity, measured yield or current NZP operating performance. No current endorsement is implied.

Other proposals in the archive are not represented as completed facilities. Confidential correspondence, pricing disputes and allegations from the source narratives are excluded from the customer-facing page.

### Record boundaries and reconciliations

- Lancaster: 1 MW furnace; metal recovery and sale, combusted offgas, historical mothballing and dismantling. No unsupported present operating status.
- Huntsville: copper-electrode AC torch, not the current NZP graphite-arc configuration. The separate LLNL 1999–2000 test system is explicitly identified as separate equipment (up to 150 kg/h in SUI #1).
- Taipei: SUI #2 and the retrospective disagree on dates and installation duration. No exact operating dates or construction duration published. The chemical-feedstock use of syngas is stated in both records. Typhoon damage retained as historical outcome; no assertion of current operation.
- Shanghai: published power and throughput figures conflict between PEAT and the case-study overview. Those numbers are omitted; acceptance-test dates, medical feedstock and stainless-steel recovery are retained. No current operating status asserted.
- California: 1 MW and dust vitrification from project 2; date not invented.
- Birmingham ASR: confidential customer not named. Proposed 5 MW conversion and 5 short tons/h design basis retained; inconsistent power-output figures, historical financial projections and current legal conclusions omitted.
- Jasper: project ended in 2004; multiple historical feedstock/equipment configurations exist. Conflicting furnace ratings and power units omitted. Local integration and development work retained.
- UAB: development concept and MOU, not a built plant. The summary’s ambiguous “completed” label is not interpreted as commissioning. Proposed 0.5 short tons/h retained; inconsistent torch/furnace descriptions are not resolved by assertion.
- Misty Mountain: earlier 10 MW / 12 short tons/h design distinguished from the later INL 500 metric tonnes/day model. FEL-1/FEL-2 and funding outcome described without allegations about individuals.
- Chester: proposed multiple 25 MW trains; engineering and logistics work described as historical development. No completed plant, current commercial partnership, present offtake or financing claim.
- Hanjung: primary brochure adds the six-plus-two surrogate-test programme, melt temperature, product testing and plant systems. MTI-500 photographic labels remain distinct from HVP200 brochure specifications.
- INL: waste composition is stated by mass in the original model; equilibrium assumption and exclusion of downstream FT synthesis are retained. No current production, current economics, performance guarantee or institutional endorsement inferred.

Original documents, authenticated links, contracts, private contacts, restricted EPC text and internal assessment/checklist material are not published.

## Independent industrial precedents — additional review

Removed the numerical overview strip (project count, country count and value-pathway count) in favour of the project material itself. Retained equipment ratings and dated technical facts within records.

Added a separate customer-facing section of independent industrial examples. None is attributed to NZP, represented as a use of NZP proprietary technology, or added to a claimed NZP project count.

- **Elkem Salten:** `ELKEM Norway SAF 729-Kamfjord.pdf`, read from the independent-report archive. INFACON XII 2010 paper, measurement campaign April 2009, 45 MW furnace. Distinguishes existing cooling-water uses from proposed offgas power recovery. Public original: https://www.pyrometallurgy.co.za/InfaconXII/729-Kamfjord.pdf
- **CC Metals and Alloys:** archive folder `CC Metals`, `Executive Summary.pdf` (2 August 2024) and `Statement of Basis and Summary.pdf` (25 March 2024), Kentucky permit V-23-022. Three submerged-arc furnaces, ferrosilicon, open configuration, hoods and baghouses. Used as an equipment/process precedent, without a current compliance, emissions-performance or sealed-gas recovery claim.
- **Felman Production:** `felman production air permit.pdf` in the corresponding archive folder is actually a February 2012 Title V renewal application. Three ratings (32/20/20 MW) cross-checked against the 29 August 2014 EPA memorandum, section 3, publicly available at https://downloads.regulations.gov/EPA-HQ-OAR-2010-0895-0175/content.pdf . The archived application contains inconsistent ferroalloy product descriptions in forms; the page does not repeat these product details. No present operating-status assertion.
- **Albany:** `Plasma arc melter waste to value products_Eddy_1996.pdf` describes ASME/USBM ash vitrification, the three-phase graphite arc melter, and subsequent DOE/USBM work on combustible wastes. Supporting Bureau of Mines 1995 facility-design record read at https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/237729 . Described as research programmes, not a commercial NZP installation. Different test equipment ratings and tonnage conventions across reports are not combined into a single specification.
- **Jindal Angul:** `Coal Gas for DRI plant.pdf`, archived operator case study, explains coal-derived gas, CO/H2 use for iron reduction, gas cleaning and by-products. Confirmed operator's published facility description at https://www.jindalsteel.in/odisha . Identified as downstream syngas use; no claim of NZP supply, plasma conversion or waste-derived feedstock. No carbon-neutrality claim repeated from archived copy.
- **Wuhan:** `Wuhan Iron & Steel _ GE Vernova.pdf`, archived operator case study captured April 2025. Public original read at https://www.gevernova.com/gas-power/resources/case-studies/wuhan . Gas-to-power and steam integration only; blast-furnace gas explicitly distinguished from intended waste-derived syngas. Avoids projecting the supplier's savings/performance figures onto NZP.

Other records inspected included ABB ArcSave at SDI Roanoke, Tenova CONSTEERRER, Globe Metallurgical legal filings, the general plasma-gasification council paper and the industrial CO techno-economic research paper. Not added merely to increase the apparent portfolio: some concern different proprietary equipment, conceptual research or compliance/legal records that do not directly demonstrate a relevant NZP process configuration. The scanned Bridgeport/Hamilton files did not yield extractable source content and were not used to substantiate new cases.
