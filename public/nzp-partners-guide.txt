NET ZERO PLATFORMS — PARTNERS & LICENSING

Feedstock owners, customers, technology suppliers and delivery partners each bring a different part of the project. Define the responsibilities before committing capital.

FEEDSTOCK & OFFTAKE PARTNERS
Build a dependable supply and product-demand case with the organisations that own the materials and use the outputs.
• Volume, quality and contract duration
• Preparation and delivery responsibilities
• Product specification and acceptance

TECHNOLOGY LICENSING
For qualified opportunities, discuss the scope of NZP technology and know-how within an integrated project.
• Technology scope and permitted use
• Territory, documentation and interfaces
• Commercial terms and performance responsibilities

EQUIPMENT & SYNTHESIS SUPPLIERS
Select compatible furnace, gas-processing, generation and synthesis equipment around a defined design basis.
• Equipment interfaces and guarantees
• Gas specification and catalyst protection
• Commissioning and acceptance tests

ENGINEERING & DELIVERY PARTNERS
Align design, permitting, procurement, construction and commissioning with clear interface ownership.
• Design authority and work packages
• Programme, permits and utilities
• Site integration and handover

REGIONAL & CHANNEL PARTNERS
Discuss origination and local delivery around a specific market, customer need and available capabilities.
• Region and customer access
• Technical and commercial capability
• Responsibilities and project governance

OPERATIONS & SUPPORT
Plan operating capability, maintenance, training and escalation as part of development.
• Operator training and documentation
• Maintenance and spare-parts strategy
• Service scope and operating responsibilities

Partner appointments, licensing rights, territory, commercial terms and performance obligations are defined by project-specific agreements. No supplier endorsement or signed partnership is implied by these role descriptions.
