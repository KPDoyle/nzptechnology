NET ZERO PLATFORMS
PROJECT SCREENING GUIDE — OCTOBER 2026

Waste-to-Value starts where recycling stops.

1. THE STARTING POINT
Organisation, project contact and location.
The problem to solve and intended output.
Project timing and site status.

2. FEEDSTOCK
Residual type and annual volume; seasonal availability.
Current destination and treatment cost.
Representative composition, moisture, ash and calorific value.
Contaminants and any hazardous classification.
Existing analysis, sampling method and variability.
Preparation and transport requirements.

3. SITE AND OUTPUT
Available land, electricity, water and access.
Planning and environmental constraints.
Electricity and useful heat demand profiles.
Hydrogen, CO or syngas volume, purity, pressure and delivery conditions.
Potential downstream synthesis partner and offtake requirement.

4. DEVELOPMENT PATHWAY
SCREEN: define materials, baseline, local demand and missing information.
MODEL: compare process architectures and sensitivities.
VALIDATE: feedstock, mass/energy balance, emissions, residues and product specification.
DEVELOP: site, permits, contracts, delivery interfaces and investment case.
DEPLOY: integrate, commission and verify agreed acceptance criteria.

5. EVIDENCE CHECKLIST
Established component maturity is distinct from integrated project performance.
Historical operating evidence must identify the system, application and period.
Models must identify assumptions and be reviewed against measured data.
Net energy includes internal consumption; output pathways cannot double-count syngas.
Carbon assessment must define the baseline and whole-system boundary.
Byproducts require testing and acceptance; reuse is not automatic.
Commercial returns and carbon credit eligibility need project-specific substantiation.

This guide is explanatory, not a performance specification or investment forecast.
