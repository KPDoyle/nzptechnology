NET ZERO PLATFORMS — INDUSTRIES / MARKETS

Explore where qualified residual resources, useful energy and industrial molecule demand can meet. Each market starts with its own specification and commercial baseline.

STEEL & METALS
Assess carbon-bearing residuals, useful heat and reducing-gas requirements alongside the site’s existing process. Process-gas reuse needs a separate composition and integration assessment.
• Residual and process-gas composition
• Furnace interfaces and utilities
• Product demand and operating profile

GLASS & CERAMICS
Explore controllable energy and process heat around a demanding continuous production environment. Gas quality, burner compatibility and heat delivery determine the fit.
• Temperature and heat-demand profile
• Fuel specification and burner compatibility
• Continuity and backup supply

CHEMICAL MANUFACTURING
Screen local hydrogen, carbon monoxide or synthesis-gas supply against the specification of an existing or proposed chemical process.
• Purity, pressure and contaminant limits
• Hourly and annual molecule demand
• Conditioning, storage and offtake

FUELS & SUSTAINABLE AVIATION FUEL
Consider syngas preparation for specialist downstream synthesis, including Fischer–Tropsch pathways. Fuel eligibility and certification must be established for the particular feedstock and route.
• Downstream licensor specification
• Feedstock origin and carbon assessment
• Certification, scale and product offtake

WASTE & RESOURCE MANAGEMENT
Connect qualified non-recyclable residuals with a useful product pathway after practical reuse and recycling. Start with the current treatment cost and supply profile.
• Composition and seasonality
• Current destination and treatment costs
• Preparation, permits and residues

REGIONAL DEVELOPERS & INDUSTRIAL ESTATES
Bring local residual supply, available utilities and nearby demand into a phased project. The industrial and community setting shapes the development pathway.
• Site and infrastructure
• Supply and customer relationships
• Planning, logistics and development phasing

These are project applications to assess, rather than a list of completed NZP installations.
