NET ZERO PLATFORMS — TECHNOLOGY & IP

NZP’s proposition combines high-temperature conversion principles with gas-processing interfaces, configuration models and project engineering.

CONVERSION ARCHITECTURE
Submerged-arc furnace principles form the starting point for controlled high-temperature conversion of qualified material.
• Electrical load and operating conditions
• Feedstock preparation and furnace design
• Electrodes, refractory and residue handling

GAS PROCESSING & INTEGRATION
Industrial cleaning, conditioning and separation connect raw gas with the selected output route.
• Contaminant load and cleaning stages
• Mass and energy balance
• Downstream equipment specification

CONFIGURATION MODELS
Engineering and commercial models compare routes around a defined feedstock, site and offtake requirement.
• Model version and assumptions
• Measured inputs and calibration
• Uncertainty and independent review

DRAWINGS & TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION
A project evidence pack can include the design basis, engineering drawings, equipment interfaces and operating procedures appropriate to its stage.
• Documentation scope and ownership
• Revision control and design approval
• Access, confidentiality and permitted use

IP & LICENSING REVIEW
Establish the rights needed for the proposed implementation, including relevant NZP know-how and third-party process technology.
• Patent and application records where applicable
• Ownership and permitted use
• Third-party licences and freedom-to-operate review

TRAINING & KNOWLEDGE TRANSFER
Define the operating knowledge, training material and support needed for a safe handover.
• Training scope and operator competence
• Manuals and maintenance procedures
• Acceptance and ongoing support

Patent ownership, applications, licence scope and third-party rights should be established through the relevant records and agreements. This page makes no claim to a granted patent portfolio.
