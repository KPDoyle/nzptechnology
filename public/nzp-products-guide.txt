NET ZERO PLATFORMS — PRODUCTS & APPLICATIONS

A syngas intermediate creates options. The practical output depends on gas quality, processing equipment, local demand and the economics of the complete route.

SYNGAS
A hydrogen- and carbon-monoxide-containing intermediate for energy or further processing. Its composition must suit the intended application.
• H₂ / CO ratio and gas composition
• Contaminant limits and pressure
• Continuous supply requirement

POWER & USEFUL HEAT
Configure qualified, cleaned gas for compatible engines or turbines and assess useful heat recovery against actual site demand.
• Net exported electricity after internal loads
• Heat temperature, volume and timing
• Equipment specification and backup

HYDROGEN
Consider separation and conditioning for industrial use, fuel cells or other defined demand. Product classification depends on the feedstock, electricity and applicable methodology.
• Purity and pressure
• Recovery and conditioning duties
• Storage, distribution and carbon intensity

CARBON MONOXIDE
Assess specified CO for chemical synthesis. Product purity, conditioning and safe industrial handling are central to the route.
• CO purity and impurity limits
• Separation and compression
• Customer interface and supply volume

FISCHER–TROPSCH FUELS & SAF
Prepare suitable synthesis gas for specialist fuel synthesis. Aviation-fuel pathways need their own upgrading, approval and eligibility assessment.
• Synthesis licensor requirements
• Upgrading and product specification
• Carbon intensity and certification

METHANOL & CHEMICAL INTERMEDIATES
Consider syngas as an input to downstream synthesis. Acetic acid and formic acid involve additional process steps and a separate commercial case.
• Synthesis route and catalyst protection
• Downstream equipment and utilities
• Customer demand and product specification

AMMONIA, FERTILISER & MARINE-FUEL PATHWAYS
Evaluate hydrogen supply alongside nitrogen production, synthesis and the intended downstream market. These are integrated project routes.
• Hydrogen and nitrogen requirements
• Synthesis scale and energy use
• Storage, logistics and product eligibility

RECOVERED METALS & VITRIFIED MATERIAL
Investigate solid products through composition, recovery, leachability and customer acceptance testing. A usable product route must be demonstrated.
• Material composition and recovery
• Leachability and product standards
• Market acceptance and permitted use

Downstream products are potential pathways requiring specialist synthesis technology and project validation. NZP does not claim to produce every product listed in one standard plant.
