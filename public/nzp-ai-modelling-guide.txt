NET ZERO PLATFORMS — AI & MODELLING

Explore the modelling work behind project selection: material balances, net energy, product routes, commercial sensitivity and lifecycle carbon.

FEEDSTOCK-TO-OUTPUT CONFIGURATION
Compare viable processing routes using representative material data and customer output specifications.
• Feedstock chemistry and variability
• Process assumptions and equipment limits
• Product specification and downstream route

MASS & ENERGY BALANCES
Account for utilities, internal electricity, gas conditioning, downstream conversion and usable products across the full boundary.
• Purchased energy and internal loads
• Gas, water and solid material flows
• Net exported energy and useful heat

COMMERCIAL SENSITIVITY
Test treatment income, product sales, operating costs and capital assumptions separately. Use the live illustrative calculator as a starting point.
• Contracted net treatment revenue
• Saleable product volume and price
• Capital, availability and operating costs

CARBON ASSESSMENT
Compare the complete project with a defined baseline and distinguish fossil from biogenic material.
• Feedstock origin and baseline
• Electricity, transport and downstream use
• Capture, storage and assessment method

AI-ASSISTED RESEARCH & DOCUMENTATION
Potential uses include organising technical literature, extracting assumptions and checking documentation consistency, with expert review.
• Source traceability and confidentiality
• Human review of generated material
• Versioning and limits of the tool

PROCESS OPTIMISATION & MODEL INTEGRATION
Define the appropriate interface between engineering models, equipment suppliers and operating data during development.
• Available data and measurement quality
• Model validation and update process
• Control boundaries and safety responsibilities

The live calculator uses transparent arithmetic. This website does not run an AI engineering model, predict feedstock yields or provide an automated investment decision. Engineering results require a documented model and review.
