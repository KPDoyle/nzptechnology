• Representative sampling
• Moisture, ash and contaminants

PRODUCT & OFFTAKE CHECKLIST
Define the customer requirement before selecting the downstream route.
• Purity, pressure and delivery
• Volume and operating profile
• Contract and acceptance requirements

PARTNER & LICENSING DISCUSSION GUIDE
Set out scope, responsibilities and interfaces for a collaborative project.
• Technology and territory
• Engineering and equipment interfaces
• Commercial responsibilities and support

RESEARCH COLLABORATION BRIEF
Frame a useful research question and the evidence it should produce.
• Problem and proposed method
• Data access and measurable outcomes
• Publication and ownership

MODEL ASSUMPTIONS CHECKLIST
Record a model’s inputs, boundaries and limits before relying on its outputs.
• Source data and version
• Net energy and saleable volumes
• Sensitivity, uncertainty and validation

These are introductory NZP guides, not plant specifications, operating manuals or validated performance reports. Project-specific documents and confidential records are discussed separately.
